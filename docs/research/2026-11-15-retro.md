# Retro: desktop-calls, 2026-11-15 01:00Z to 2026-11-16 00:45Z

For the chief of staff, who decides. Nothing here was filed, changed or pursued.

**Headline.** The sitting failed three times for one reason: every part of the first-run path (the Simulated Phone's pairing, the Contacts prompt, the checklist wizard's steps) was built from documentation and inference, never from a recording of the real Pixel, and nothing in the environment stopped "agent-side complete" being declared on top of that. The second headline: CI was rerun 10 times in the window, not the 4 the wiki records, and the no-rerun rule is not in the in-repo implementer brief at all.

**Window in numbers (verified with `gh` and the transcript):** 59 PRs merged, 48 issues created (7 open), 24 agents spawned, 10 CI runs at attempt 2, 18 harness security warnings (15 "Merge Without Review"), 107 task notifications to the chief of staff of which 106 were interim "waiting on its own background work", and about 56 of the chief of staff's 121 messages to the owner were short no-news status lines.

**Sources.** Chief of staff transcript lines 4119 to 8045 (cited as `T<line>`); subagent transcripts by agent id; `gh` for issues #372 to #392, PRs #374 to #391 and the Actions runs; `docs/wiki/` and `docs/agents/` at `9479676`; `crates/simulated-phone/src`, `crates/automation`, `tools/`, `justfile`. The research folder holds 268 `[I]` markers; none is tracked as an open question with an owner.

### B. The checklist wizard failed the owner three times

**B1 [V]. The wizard was proven only from a clean start on the Simulated Phone.** Before #383, group 1 had one test, `group_1_pairs_afresh_to_a_first_call_lets_the_contacts_prompt_time_out_...`: a fresh Phone, one run, the prompt the stand-in invents. A real sitting starts dirty: the Pixel still paired, a pairing made on the Phone's side, a results file from a run a product bug cut short. `an_interrupted_run_resumes_at_the_failed_step_and_keeps_what_passed` tested the very blending #380 reports, as the intended behaviour.

**B2 [V]. The fix repeated the fault.** #383's body says, under design decisions: "**[I]** Inferred, not checked on the real Pixel (the owner is mid-sitting): the box's wording, and whether the unticked Pixel asks or refuses. 1.3 handles all three outcomes, so the next run settles it." The owner was sent back in at T7580 without the caveat that 1.3 rested on an inference.

**B3 [V]. A wrong expectation in the wizard costs the owner a full unpair and re-pair.** The wizard has two verdicts. When the Phone does something the step did not expect, it fails the step, although the app behaved correctly both times (#380's pairing, #392's "isn't sharing contacts yet" page). Cost to the owner: three reports (T7023, T7228, T7910), three hours (21:38Z to 00:38Z), at least four pairings, and group 1 of 7 still not passed.

**B4 [I]. The wizard and the stand-in share one author's assumptions, so testing one against the other proves little.** Steps 1.2 to 1.4 were written from the same research note that shaped the stand-in. A demo run passes by construction.
