# Earbuds beside the Desktop

Date: 2026-11-05. Ticket #13, part 2 (scenario 20). Only the Simulated Phone was used: the real earbuds are left to Real-Phone Checklist group 5 (#29), and what that sitting should expect is posted there.
Legend: **[V]** = verified, against the Simulated Phone or in AOSP source as stated. **[I]** = inferred, not tested.

**Bottom line:** the app needed no change for earbuds. When the Phone keeps the audio on them, the Handoff to the Desktop from part 1 retries once and then shows its plain message. The Simulated Phone now models earbuds the way AOSP routes hands-free audio, and 4 of 4 new scenarios pass.

## 1. What Android does

- **[V]** (AOSP source, `packages/modules/Bluetooth`, `main`) Android sends Call audio to one active hands-free device. `HeadsetService.isScoAcceptable` returns `ERROR_NOT_ACTIVE_DEVICE` for any other device.
- **[V]** (same) `HeadsetSystemInterface.answerCall(device)` makes the answering device the active one, unless that device's call-establish audio policy is `POLICY_NOT_ALLOWED`. `HeadsetService.dialOutgoingCall` does the same for a dial.
- **[I]** Newly connected earbuds become the active device. That would come from `ActiveDeviceManager`, which I didn't read.
- **[I]** PipeWire replies to `Activate` before the Phone acts, so a Phone that refuses audio to the Desktop looks the same as one that ignores `Activate`.

## 2. The Simulated Phone's earbuds

- `SetEarbuds(true)` connects them, and they become the active device. While they are active, `Activate` does nothing, and a Call placed on the Phone or answered on the earbuds keeps its audio there.
- The Desktop's `Answer` or `Dial` makes the Desktop the active device, unless the new quirk `earbuds-keep-audio` is on. That quirk stands for a `POLICY_NOT_ALLOWED` audio policy, or any Phone that holds on to the earbuds.

## 3. Scenarios

`crates/test-harness/tests/earbuds.rs`, 4 of 4 pass. **[V]**
- With the earbuds connected, the Banner appears, and answering on the Desktop ends with "Audio on this computer".
- With `earbuds-keep-audio`, answering on the Desktop ends with "Your phone kept the audio; to hear the Call here, tap the audio button on your phone's call screen." The `audio-not-moved` error is raised, the Call stays live, and the Phone is never asked to hang up.
- A Call answered on the earbuds shows "Audio on phone" and "Move audio here". Then `MoveAudio("desktop")` ends in the same plain message, with the Call still live.
